# Optional Real-World Test Photos

For a stronger camera/classification demo, use real road photos in addition to the clean Wikimedia sign graphics.

- Stop sign photo (Unsplash): https://unsplash.com/photos/a-stop-sign-on-a-road-Cz2ILiieMtQ
- Speed Limit 35 photo (Unsplash): https://unsplash.com/photos/a-speed-limit-sign-on-the-side-of-the-road-kO54ZpAi-5U
- Speed Limit 45 photo (Unsplash): https://unsplash.com/photos/a-road-with-a-bridge-over-it-SSS2rR38hrY

These photos are marked free to use under the Unsplash License on their source pages. Download them from the source pages, save them inside `demo_images/`, and pass the correct label to `offline_fgsm_demo.py`.
